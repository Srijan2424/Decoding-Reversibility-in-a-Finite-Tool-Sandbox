# Restricted ScienceWorld recovery benchmark

This pilot uses kitchen-start variation 2 of boil, freeze, chemistry-mix and melt. It selects three initial candidates before running recovery: open the cupboard, activate the stopwatch and eat the apple. Recovery is limited to open cupboard, close cupboard, activate stopwatch, deactivate stopwatch, eat apple and wait1. At each node only the exact command strings advertised as valid by the simulator are used. Keys are literal commands, and object-bearing commands require a unique binding with the intended object UUID at the root. The horizon counts further calls, including wait1 as one call. Terminal branches end.

The observation tracks the original kitchen cupboard's open flag, the original kitchen apple's presence and containment path, the original stopwatch's activation and current/displayed time, and the kitchen air temperature. It preserves original object identity for the named objects. Exact recovery restores every compared field. Partial recovery restores every compared field except kitchen air temperature. Irreversible means neither kind was found after enumerating every path at that horizon. Cases where the candidate does not change the comparison, a replay disagrees, or a required field is missing are excluded. The experiment records limits of one, two and three further calls. The conditions apply to the restricted command list and field projection. The pilot deliberately accepts a small sample in place of the original 800-example target.
